About SpeakSafe

Most harassment goes unreported. We want to change that.

Many people stay silent because they fear retaliation, losing a job or grades, or not being believed. SpeakSafe gives survivors and witnesses a safe first step: record what happened, understand their rights, and reach people who can help.

Unwanted touching or groping
Sexual comments, jokes or gestures
Demands for sex in exchange for grades, jobs or favours
Sending or sharing sexual images
Stalking, following or repeated messages
Sexual assault and rape

If you were assaulted

  • Get somewhere safe first, then call someone you trust.
  • Visit a sexual assault referral centre within 72 hours for emergency contraception and HIV prevention medicine.
  • If you can, keep the clothes you wore in a paper bag and avoid washing before a medical exam.
§

Know your rights

  • The Violence Against Persons (Prohibition) Act 2015 criminalises rape, sexual assault and other violence.
  • Many universities and employers have sexual harassment policies. Ask for them in writing.
  • You can report to the police, the NHRC or a state agency. You do not need a lawyer to start.

Keep a record

  • Write down dates, places, what was said and who was nearby.
  • Screenshot messages, including the sender's profile and the time.
  • Store copies somewhere the other person cannot reach.

You do not have to carry this alone.
