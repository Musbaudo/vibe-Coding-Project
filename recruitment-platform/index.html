How It Works

From role brief to first day on the job

The same six stages for every hire, local or international, so you always know where each candidate stands.

STAGE 01

Role brief

We agree headcount, skills, location, salary band and start dates with you.

STAGE 02

Sourcing

We campaign through trade schools, workshops, tech communities, professional bodies and our candidate database.

STAGE 03

Screening & testing

Practical trade tests or engineer-led technical assessments, plus credential and reference checks.

STAGE 04

Interview & selection

You interview the shortlist in person or by video. We handle scheduling and feedback.

STAGE 05

Onboarding or relocation

Local hires start straight away. For overseas roles we prepare documents with your licensed migration agent.

STAGE 06

Aftercare

Check-ins through the first months to support retention for you and settling in for them.
