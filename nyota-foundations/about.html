Two faiths, one hand extended

We are Nigerians who believe that mercy is bigger than the things that divide us.

Our story

Born from a shared refusal to look away

Nyota Foundations began when a group of Muslim and Christian neighbours in northern Nigeria watched families arrive with nothing after their villages were attacked. Instead of asking who should help, they simply started helping — together.

What started as food shared from a few kitchens has grown into an organised response: shelter, nutrition, trauma care and the slow, patient work of helping families return home and rebuild. We remain volunteer-rooted, community-led and answerable to the people we serve.

Our mission

To shelter, feed and restore Nigerian families displaced by banditry and kidnapping, regardless of faith or background.

👁

Our vision

A Nigeria where no displaced family is left without shelter, and where faith unites communities rather than divides them.

Our values

Compassion without conditions, transparency with every naira, and deep respect for the dignity of every person we serve.

Standing together

A word on why Muslims and Christians serve as one

Both of our traditions place mercy and care for the stranger at their heart. When violence tries to set neighbour against neighbour, our shared answer is to serve side by side — publicly, joyfully and without preference. That unity is not a slogan for us. It is how the work gets done.
