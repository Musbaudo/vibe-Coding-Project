What we do

Care for the whole family, from the first night onward

🍽

Food & clean water

Emergency food parcels and safe water for families arriving at camps with nothing but the clothes they wear.

🏠

Safe shelter

Tents, bedding and dignified temporary housing so no child sleeps exposed to the elements or to danger.

❤

Trauma & healing

Counselling and prayer support — from imams and pastors alike — for those who have lost family to violence.

📚

Children's schooling

Learning spaces and materials so displaced children keep growing while the crisis is resolved.

🤝

Family reunification

Tracing and reuniting relatives separated during attacks, abductions and hurried evacuations.

🌱

Rebuilding livelihoods

Seeds, small grants and skills training that help families stand on their own feet again back home.

One Nigeria · Two faiths · One purpose

Why we stand together

Bandits divide communities. We refuse to be divided.

The crisis does not ask a family's religion before it takes their home. Neither do we before we help. Nyota means "star" — the light that guides travellers home. Muslim and Christian, we follow the same light: mercy for whoever needs it most.

01

Help by need, never by creed

Aid is given to whoever is most vulnerable — Muslim, Christian or neither.

02

Led by both communities

Our volunteers and boards include imams, pastors and lay leaders working as one.

03

Accountable and transparent

Every naira is tracked and reported, so donors and communities can trust the work.

Kaduna corridor · 2025

A family we walked with

“They fled at midnight. By morning, they had a tent, a meal and someone who prayed with them.”

When Amina and her four children reached the camp after their village was raided, they had walked for two days. Within hours our team had shelter and food ready. Six months on, the children are back in a learning space and Amina sells vegetables again.
